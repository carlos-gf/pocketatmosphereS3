Pocket Atmosphere

Six atmospheric fields in the hand, and one dial that decides how much of each one is left. For the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.69.

⚠️ Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

The board holds one firmware at a time, so installing replaces whatever is on it. Install without erasing and the words and dwell log already collected survive.

Holding it

drag up / downreduction depth, live under the thumb
swipe sidewaysnext field — each remembers its own depth
tapshow or hide the readout
hold about a secondname what you're looking at
left button, short pressnext screen
left button, hold ~1.5 spower off properly

Over USB at 115200 baud: DUMP for the words as CSV, BAND for the dwell histogram, SOURCES if you really want to know where the fields came from.

Pick the build that matches your board. Both are ESP32-S3, so nothing stops you flashing the wrong one \u2014 it just won\u2019t come up. Re-flash the right one and it recovers.
